# How I want my inbox triaged

Everything in this folder is invented for testing ElicitAgent. Every person, address and email is fictional.

## Labels

Give every email exactly one of these four labels:

- Urgent
- Reply this week
- Read later
- Archive

## My rules

- Anything from my landlord, Mira Landlordson, is Urgent.
- Bills that are due within 7 days are Urgent.
- Newsletters go to Read later.
- Anything from my sister Tess is Reply this week.
- Book club meets on Thursdays. Anything about book club is Reply this week.
- Never delete anything.
- Do not send replies for me. You can draft a reply and I will send it.

## Notes

- The inbox export is emails.csv. It covers the last two weeks.
- Choir emails come from the Harmony Lane Choir and are usually about rehearsals.
