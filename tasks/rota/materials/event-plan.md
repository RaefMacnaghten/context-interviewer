# Larkfield Lane Folk Afternoon: event plan

Everything in this folder is invented for testing ElicitAgent. The event, the hall and every person are fictional.

We run the Folk Afternoon every year. This is the plan for this year's.

## The day

- Date: Saturday 6 March 2027
- Place: Larkfield Community Hall, Larkfield Lane
- Setup starts at 12:00. Doors open at 14:00.
- Music runs from 14:30 to 20:30, five acts with short changeovers.
- Pack down runs from 20:30 to 22:30.
- We expect about 150 people.

## What I need

Please make the volunteer rota for the day and send it to everyone.

We usually do 2-hour shifts. The roles and rules are in roles-and-rules.md and the people who signed up are in volunteers.csv.

## Budget

There is no budget for paid staff. Every role is filled by volunteers.
