# Roles and rules

## Roles

- Door: 2 people at all times while the doors are open.
- Bar: 2 people from 14:00 to 20:30.
- Stage crew: 2 people for setup and for pack down, 1 person while the music is on.
- Raffle: 1 person from 16:00 to 18:00.
- First aid: 1 qualified first aider.

## Rules

- Everyone on the bar must be over 18.
- At least one personal licence holder must be on every bar shift.
- A qualified first aider must be on site whenever the public are in the hall.
- Nobody works more than 4 hours in total, except team leads.
- Everyone gets a 30-minute break.
- Only give people roles they signed up for.
