## Use of AI, in full

AI assistance (Claude, via Claude Code) was used for design discussion, drafting and, for this build, writing the code.

**The design.** The ideas began from the author's own description of the tool and how he wanted it to work. The AI drafted lines from that description; the author refined, edited or accepted each over several rounds, with his own input on how it should work. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md) gives the detail, entry by entry.

What was the author's: the idea (entry 1); the problem-agnostic scope (entry 2); the process, which settled design intent by questions before any screens and made the question board adapt to his answers (entries 4 and 12); the challenges that changed seven of the nine design lines (entries 14 to 20); and his corrections of 24 Nov 2026 (entries 21 to 30), among them that reading the folder comes before the rule, that "Nobody can answer this" was a false label, that the step where the person states their intent was missing, and the name.

What was the AI's: the drafting and wording of the design and the plan; the items it raised and he confirmed (the named-person rule, the targeted second look, the fifth state now named "Not available here" and first called "cannot be done", the two yes-or-no harm checks, and the third rule, the skip button, the source expansion and the "why this question" panel, listed in the log's closing section); and the code.

In his words:

> all lines / ideas were drafted by the AI from my initial description of the tool and how I wanted it to work.

> it was an iterative process for all of the points, right? I gave a lot of my own personal input on how I wanted it to be so it was drafted by AI and refined and edited, and/or accepted by me, depending on the quality of the AI's proposition.

The wording of the plan's 14 points (P1 to P14) is the AI's, not the author's. P1, P3, P4 and P14 were settled in discussion with him; the others were the AI's proposals, which he approved with the plan, with the two changes above. [docs/decisions.md](decisions.md) records each early decision with its date, what the AI recommended, what the author decided in his own words, and which parts were original and which were AI-assisted. The log itself was drafted with AI assistance from the author's verbatim messages.

**The code.** The code, tests and task materials in this repository were written by an AI coding agent (Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code) from the author's approved plan, on 24 Nov 2026. The two answer sheets were drafted separately, by an independent AI agent that saw only the materials, the intent line, the headings and the column definitions (see "Task folders" in [how-it-works.md](how-it-works.md)); they are drafts for the author to review, edit and lock.

**At run time.** The tool itself calls a language model (`claude-sonnet-5-5` by default) to list items and to make the estimates. The ask table, not the model, decides what to do.
