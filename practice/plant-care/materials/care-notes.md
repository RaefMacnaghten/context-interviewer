# My plant notes

- Monstera: every 10 days in winter. Turn it a quarter each week so it grows straight.
- Fern: mist daily; how much water: ask me.
- Peace lily: it droops when it's thirsty, so water it when it droops and not before.
- Orchid: ask me. I keep changing my mind about the orchid.
- Feeding: the green bottle under the sink, a capful in a full can. Not in winter except
  the ones marked monthly.
- Never water the cactuses in winter.
- Don't throw anything out, even if it looks dead. The calathea always looks dead in
  November and comes back. Ask me before anything goes in the compost, once it's gone it's gone.
- Use the watering can, not a glass, it's easier to see how much went in.
- Juno has a key. Mrs Pell at number 6 has the spare.
