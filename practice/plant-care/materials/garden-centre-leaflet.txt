Brambleby Garden Centre: houseplant care in winter (leaflet, 2022)

Most houseplants need less water from November to February.
- Snake plant: water every two weeks.
- Aloe: water every three weeks, let it dry right out.
- Orchids: soak the pot for ten minutes once a week, then drain.
- Venus flytraps: rainwater or distilled water only, never tap.
- Ferns: keep the soil moist and the air humid.
- Spider plants: water when the top of the soil is dry.
- Herbs indoors: water every other day, don't let them sit in water.
- Feed only actively growing plants.
Ask our staff about frost protection for outdoor pots.
