# Making the rota (Hamish)

## When and where
- I post the rota in the School Run chat on Sunday evening.
- I always end with "Thumbs up when you've seen it please".
- After posting I add the week to past-rotas.csv. Every week since September 2025.

## How it looks
- Usually a little table: day, morning driver, afternoon driver, late pick-ups. (On my phone twice I just did a list.)
- First names only for the kids, there's only one of each.
- I put the after-school clubs in so nobody forgets a late pick-up (forgot netball twice in October, sorry Isla).
- Morning route is usually Zara first, then the Green. When Kofi drives he sometimes does the Green first and gets Zara after, which is fine, it's his car.

## Things I check
- Who has the Friday this week (see the agreement).
- Is Isla's netball on? It gets cancelled now and then, the club texts on the day.
- Is Nell's piano on? Cancelled a couple of times this term, Jo usually says.
- Zara: Reception door at 3:05. Twice this term Priya was at work late and Zara stayed at Little Acorns till 5.
- Rory's inhaler stays in Rory's bag. (Once it ended up in my glovebox for a week.)
- Seats: count the boosters against the kids in each car.
