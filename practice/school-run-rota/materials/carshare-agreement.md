# Example Lane school-run car share - how it works

Agreed by the four families in September 2025. Hamish makes the rota.

## Who drives
- Each family has a weekday: Monday Example (Hamish), Tuesday Sample (Jo), Wednesday Testwell (Kofi), Thursday Placeholder (Priya).
- Fridays go round in turn: Example, Sample, Testwell, Placeholder, then back to Example.
- The weekday driver does the morning, the afternoon and any late pick-ups that day.
- Backup drivers named on the school's list: the four parents plus Granny Moira (Example).

## Times and places
- Mornings: the car leaves the Green at 8:15. Zara is collected from home first.
- Afternoons: pick-up at the main gate at 3:15, Zara from the Reception door at 3:05. Everyone is dropped back at the Green, Zara at home.

## Seats
- Zara: high-back booster, always, in every car.
- Rory and Nell: backless booster.
- Every car carries the boosters it needs for that trip. The spare backless booster lives in Kofi's boot.

## Safety
- The driver waits at the gate until every child has been handed over to a teacher or is through the door. Nobody is left waiting.
- Children are only collected by the drivers on the school's list.

## Swaps
Swap between yourselves and tell Hamish by Saturday night so the rota is right.
