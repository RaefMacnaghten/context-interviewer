# Hall bookings - how I do the week (Fenella)

Every Friday I go through the next week's bookings, send confirmations, then send Gus (caretaker) and Hettie (cleaner) the week's list.

## Confirmations
- Subject line is always "Your booking: <day date>, <room>", e.g. "Your booking: Sat 7 Nov, Main Hall".
- I start with "Hi" and their first name for regulars, and usually "Dear" for one-offs.
- I always sign off "Fenella, Bookings, Wexample Village Hall".
- Every confirmation ends with "Please leave the chairs stacked in fives against the back wall."
- Regulars get a short reminder each week too (skipped it a couple of weeks when nothing had changed).
- One-off hirers get the hall map attached. (Forgot twice in the summer.)
- Payment: bank transfer to the hall account, reference HALL plus the date, e.g. HALL0711 for 7 November.
- One-off hirers pay a £50 refundable deposit. It's been £50 since I took over.
- One-off hirers can come in an hour early to set up, free of charge. (I've let two people have two hours when they asked nicely.)

## Keys and codes
- The key is in the keysafe by the front door. Gus changes the keysafe code on the 1st of every month and texts me the new one.
- No code until the hire fee is in. No exceptions.
- The alarm code never goes to hirers. Gus or the last regular of the day sets the alarm.
- Whoever has the last booking of the day locks up and puts the key back, except Wednesdays when Gus locks up after bridge.

## Bar
Alcohol is fine if the hirer names a personal licence holder who will be there all evening. We said no to a bar once (a 16th birthday in 2025).

## Kitchen
Children's groups don't use the hob. (We let Little Owls do pancakes once in February with Gus standing over them. Never again.)

## Cleaning
After a big party I book Hettie for 2 hours straight after, same night (3 once, after the barn dance).

## Rates
Main Hall £14 an hour, Small Room £9 an hour. Wexample Players get £10 an hour for the Main Hall.
