# How I do the flat bills (Ada)

I do this on the 1st of each month, or the next working day if the 1st is a weekend. (Did it on the 3rd in August because I was away.)

## Steps
1. Put the bills that came in last month into bills-this-month.csv.
2. Work out each share using the rules in housemates.md.
3. Post the split in the Flat 3 Bills group chat.
4. Pay anything that is due and not on direct debit from the pot.
5. Add a line per person to past-splits.csv. (Forgot in July and August, filled them in later.)

## The message
Every month the message goes like this:

    Hi all, here's the split for October. Please send your share to the pot by the 5th.
    Ada: £122.15 (energy £33.65, internet £9.00, cleaner £30.00, council tax £49.50)
    Baz: £137.15 (energy £33.65, internet £9.00, cleaner £30.00, council tax £49.50, Elin £15.00)
    Cora: £122.15 (energy £33.65, internet £9.00, cleaner £30.00, council tax £49.50)
    Dev: £122.15 (energy £33.65, internet £9.00, cleaner £30.00, council tax £49.50)
    Cheers, Ada x

I sign off "Cheers, Ada x" every time, I'm not changing that.

## Paying
- Everything goes out of the Flat 3 pot at Fernmouth Mutual. Never my own account.
- Council tax is on direct debit, never pay it by hand.
- Energy (Lumen Energy) I pay by hand on the due date, apart from August when Cora paid it on her card while I was away.
- Water (Brookvale Water) is quarterly. I've paid it by hand from the pot every quarter apart from June, when Baz paid it on his card by mistake and we had to sort out a refund. Thinking about putting it on direct debit so that can't happen again.
- If a bill is due before people have paid in, I pay on the due date anyway and let the buffer cover it. Twice I waited for everyone's money and both times it was fine, but I'd rather not risk it.
- Cleaner and window cleaner: pay by hand from the pot on the due date.

## Chasing
If someone hasn't paid by the deadline I give them a nudge. Last time Dev was late I gave Dev a nudge after three days.
