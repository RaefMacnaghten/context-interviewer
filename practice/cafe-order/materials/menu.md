# The Quillfeather Café menu (autumn)

Coffee: espresso, americano, flat white, latte, cappuccino (decaf on request)
Oat milk at no extra charge.
Hot chocolate, chai latte, tea.

Bakes (made here at weekends): scones, sausage rolls, flapjacks.
Bought in: gluten-free brownies.

Lunch: soup with bread, cheese toasties, beans on toast.

Allergen information is on the label of everything in the counter. Ask at the till.
