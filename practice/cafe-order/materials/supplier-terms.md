# Our suppliers and how they work

## Fennick Wholesale (dry goods, paper, cleaning, bought-in cakes)
- Order online by the 25th for delivery on the first Tuesday of the month.
- Minimum order £150. Once an order is confirmed it can't be changed or cancelled.
- Returns only for damaged goods. Bought-in cakes are frozen, six weeks' life once thawed.
- Printed cups are made to order with our logo: six weeks' lead time and no refunds.
  Fennick keeps a running order for us and prints from it each month.

## Thrushgill Roasters (coffee)
- Roasted to order, so beans can't be returned. Order by the 20th for the first Tuesday.
- Beans are best within four weeks of roasting.

## Dairyfold (milk, eggs, cream)
- Standing order. Changes by Thursday noon apply from the next Monday.
- Can be paused for closures with a week's notice.

Account numbers and logins are in the office folder.
