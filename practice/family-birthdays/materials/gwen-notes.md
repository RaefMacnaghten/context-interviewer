# Birthdays - how I do them (Gwen)

On the 1st I look at who has a birthday that month and get everything ordered that day.

## Cards
- All cards come from Paperlark, ordered online, posted by them.
- Cards go first class, posted at least three working days before the birthday.
- Every card is signed "Love from Gwen and Milo x". Milo doesn't mind.
- Style: Hal gets a funny one (the ruder the better), everyone else a nice normal one.
- I usually have Paperlark print the message inside. I handwrote Mum's and Grandad's in 2025 because I was seeing them anyway.
- Usually I ask Paperlark for a photo of each card before it's ordered (didn't bother in March and July).

## Presents
- Nieces and nephews: £20 each, always.
- Hal: climbing wall voucher, every year, he loves it.
- Mum: flowers delivered on the day (except 2024 when I did a garden voucher).
- Grandad: about £30. For his 80th we went to £100 and had a party.
- Jasper: money, sent by bank transfer to Jasper's saved account in my banking app (cheque once in 2024 when I'd lost the details). £25 last year.
- Ines: card only so far, she was new.
- Rowan: something small. Rowan stays at Wren's some winters (2021 and 2024), so check where to send.
- Paperlark gift wrap is £2.50 and I usually tick it.

## Never
- Never send Poppy anything to eat. Sesame allergy.

## Addresses
Saved in my Paperlark account, they've been the same for years.
