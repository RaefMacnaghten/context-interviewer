# Hollowmere Ramblers: membership rules

Adopted at the AGM, March 2024.

1. Membership lasts twelve months and ends on the last day of the month it started in.
2. Annual fees: adult £24, concession £15 (aged 65 or over, or in full-time education),
   family £40 (up to two adults and their children at the same address).
3. Honorary life members pay nothing and are never sent reminders.
4. Members are sent one reminder at the start of the month their membership ends.
5. After the end date there is a grace period of 30 days. Anyone who has not paid by
   the end of the grace period has lapsed and is removed from the walks mailing list.
6. A lapsed member can rejoin at any time by paying the fee.
7. Payments can be made by bank transfer to the club account or in cash to the
   treasurer at a walk. Cash must be handed to the treasurer, not the walk leader.
8. The membership secretary keeps the membership list. The treasurer keeps the money.
